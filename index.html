Pill Land

by Minimal Studio

A little pill at the top of your Windows screen. It shows what's playing, your timers, what's next in your day, and quick pops for volume, charging and Caps Lock. Hover it and it opens into music controls, a focus timer, a Today card and a small planner.

Everything stays on your PC. No account, no ads, no tracking.
