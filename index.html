Pill Land

Dynamic Island for Windows

A live notch at the top of your screen on Windows 10 and 11 laptops and desktops. Music, a planner, a focus timer and an app shortcut launcher, in one small pill.

Play a song and the pill shows the album art. Start a pomodoro or study timer and the countdown sits right there. Your next class or meeting can pop up with a Join button. Hover it and it opens into music controls, a Today card, habits and a small planner.

Everything stays on your PC. No account, no ads, no tracking. Pill Land is free, including Pro features during launch. Early users will keep a lower price when the monthly plan starts.

Shortcuts and the quick launcher

The Shortcuts shelf is a small desktop shortcuts organizer built into Pill Land. Drag any app, .exe, .lnk, file or folder onto the pill, or into Shortcuts in the open pill and in Settings. Pill Land saves the path and a small icon. It does not copy the program.

Open a pinned shortcut from the icon grid, or press Ctrl+Alt+Space for the quick launcher and type a name. You can rename, reorder or remove shortcuts, and they stay in your backup when you export or import your data. If a file was moved or deleted, Pill Land tells you clearly.

It is an app shortcut launcher for Windows 10 and 11, next to the planner, habit tracker, study timer, screen recorder and PDF tools.

Questions

What is Pill Land?

Pill Land is Dynamic Island for Windows. It puts a live notch at the top of your screen for music, timers, your planner and what's next in your day. It is a Windows productivity app for laptops and desktops, made by Minimal Studio.

How is it like Dynamic Island?

It sits at the top edge like a notch or a Dynamic Island. When something is going on, the pill comes alive with album art, a focus countdown, or your next class or meeting. Hover it and it opens into controls and a few useful cards.

Is Pill Land free?

Yes. Pill Land is free to use, and Pro features are free for everyone during launch. Early users will keep a lower price when the monthly plan starts.

Does it collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your PC. No account, no ads, no tracking. The only network calls are optional weather (just the city you type, sent to Open-Meteo) and, in the Microsoft Store version, the Store itself for licensing and, if you agree, a rating prompt. Read the privacy policy.

Which Windows versions does it work on?

Windows 10 (version 2004, build 19041, and later) and Windows 11, on laptops and desktops.

What is the Shortcuts shelf and the quick launcher?

You can pin apps, files and folders to a small icon grid in the pill. Drag them onto the pill or into Settings, then open them from that grid or from the Ctrl+Alt+Space quick launcher. Pill Land stores the path you chose, not a copy of the file. You can rename, reorder or remove them.

Is it part of Vedrino?

Yes. Pill Land is phase one of the Vedrino project: the Windows companion. Vedrino itself is an Android app. Pill Land is Dynamic Island for Windows.
